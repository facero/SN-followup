# SN 2026sqf 

The nearest Type Ia-CSM to date.

RA/DEC (2000)
159.6998395 +53.5094715  
Type: SN Ia-CSM  
Redshift: 0.003312  
Distance: ~19 Mpc 


Observations with XMM-Newton on October 18th 2026:

The observation was carried for 20 ks (14 ks remaining after flare filtering).
The above image is obtained with EPIC-PN camera in the 5-10 keV energy band.
The pixel size is 4.1" and a Gaussian smoothing of 8" was applied.
The 2008 image is the sum of ObsID 0556280101+0556280201.
